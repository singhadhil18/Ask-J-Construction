About Us

We are a luxury homebuilding and renovation company dedicated to creating refined, high-end spaces defined by precision, craftsmanship, and timeless design. From bespoke new builds to transformative renovations and seamless frameless showers, every project is thoughtfully executed with meticulous attention to detail.

​

As a family-run company with almost a decade of experience, our commitment to quality, sophistication, and lasting value ensures each space is as functional as it is beautiful.

Our Process

​At ASK-J Construction, our process is built around precision, transparency, and seamless execution. We believe that a successful project is not only defined by the final result, but by the experience along the way. From concept to completion, we manage every stage with professionalism and care.

Preconstruction 

​Engage with an estate approved architect and design review committee to develop plans that meet estate guidelines and client expectations.

Design & Construction Phase

​Partner with estate-approved architects to create compliant, bespoke designs.

Execute builds with high-quality materials, skilled craftsmanship and eco-friendly practices, ensuring minimal environmental impact.

QUALITY ASSURANCE 

Conduct rigorous inspections to meet NHBRC standards, estate regulations and buyer satisfaction, with at least four site visits by NHBRC inspectors.

HANDOVER 

​Deliver a flawless, fully compliant home, enrolled with the NHBRC and ready for occupancy.
